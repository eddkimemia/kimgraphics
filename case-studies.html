Fintech & Payments

SwiftPay: Redefining Mobile Trust in Kenya

The Challenge: SwiftPay launched in 2024 to simplify cross-border payments for Kenyan SMEs. Despite a superior technical product, their conversion rate was stuck at 0.8%. Users felt the brand looked "unprofessional" and were hesitant to link their bank accounts.

The Strategy: We conducted a full brand audit and identified that the original color palette (neon green) triggered "risk" associations in the financial sector. We pivoted to a Trust-Indigo and Security-Silver theme. We redesigned the landing page using F-pattern layout logic and social proof triggers above the fold.

The Technical Implementation: We built a custom React-based landing page optimized for Safaricom's network latency, reducing first-contentful-paint (FCP) from 4.2s to 1.1s. We implemented a "Trust Bar" featuring logos of partner banks and SSL badges.

+310% Signup Increase

In the first 30 days post-launch, SwiftPay saw daily signups jump from 12 to 49 per day with the same ad spend.

Detailed Analysis: By focusing on "Cognitive Ease," we simplified the multi-step signup process from 8 fields to 3, using LocalStorage to save progress. This reduced form abandonment by 65%. We also integrated a WhatsApp support bot that answered common trust-related questions in real-time.

Conclusion: Design isn't just about looks; it's about reducing friction. For SwiftPay, friction was the #1 enemy of growth. By removing it, we unlocked their true market potential.

Hospitality & Dining

The Grill Room: Dominating Nairobi's Search Map

The Challenge: A premium steakhouse in Westlands was struggling to fill tables during weekdays. Despite having 4.5 stars on TripAdvisor, they were invisible on Google Maps for "best dinner in Westlands" or "steakhouse Nairobi."

The Strategy: We implemented a "Hyper-Local SEO" strategy. This involved optimizing their Google Business Profile with professional food photography, keyword-rich descriptions, and an automated review acquisition system. We also built a mobile-optimized menu that was indexable by search engines.

Content Strategy: We produced 12 high-quality blog posts targeting long-tail keywords like "where to take a date in Nairobi" and "best private dining Westlands." This created a massive net to catch hungry searchers.

Rank #1 for 15+ Keywords

The Grill Room now appears in the "Map Pack" for all major dining searches in Nairobi, leading to 120+ additional weekly bookings.

The Result: Foot traffic increased by 45% on Mondays-Thursdays. Their website became their primary booking engine, reducing their reliance on expensive third-party delivery/booking platforms that took 20% commissions.

Key Takeaway: For local businesses in Kenya, Google Maps is the most valuable real estate you can own. We helped The Grill Room evict their competitors from that space.

E-commerce Fashion

Bloom Florals: 10x ROI via Social Commerce

The Challenge: Bloom Florals had a beautiful Instagram following but was struggling to translate "likes" into "orders." Their DM-based ordering system was chaotic, leading to lost leads and inconsistent revenue.

The Strategy: We built a custom Shopify-powered storefront integrated with M-Pesa STK Push. We replaced the manual DM process with a 1-click "Buy Now on WhatsApp" funnel. We then launched a Facebook Retargeting campaign aimed at people who added items to their cart but didn't finish the payment.

Marketing Stack: We used the Meta Pixel to track every action. We discovered that 70% of users dropped off at the "Shipping Cost" page. We advised the client to offer "Flat Rate Nairobi Shipping," which immediately boosted checkout completions by 40%.

KSh 2.4M Monthly Revenue

From a baseline of KSh 300k, Bloom Florals scaled to over KSh 2.4M in monthly sales within 5 months of our intervention.

Innovation: We implemented an "Occasion Reminder" feature. Users could opt-in to receive a WhatsApp reminder 3 days before their anniversary or a loved one's birthday with a pre-filled cart. This created a recurring revenue stream that accounted for 20% of total sales.

Outcome: Bloom Florals transformed from a small side-hustle into a fully operational business with 5 employees and a dedicated delivery fleet.

Travel & Tourism

Rift Safari Tours: Global SEO for Local Growth

The Challenge: Rift Safari Tours wanted to attract high-value European and American tourists but were being outbid on Google Ads by massive international aggregators with multi-million dollar budgets.

The Strategy: We focused on "Authority SEO" and "Content Clusters." Instead of trying to rank for "Kenya Safari" (too competitive), we targeted "7-day luxury Masai Mara safari itinerary" and "best time for Great Migration 2025." We produced 25 deep-dive guides (2,000+ words each) that established them as the ultimate experts.

Link Building: We secured mentions in travel blogs and local news outlets, boosting their domain authority from 12 to 38. We also optimized their site for "Featured Snippets," capturing the top spot on Google for dozens of "how-to" and "best-of" travel queries.

85% Organic Lead Growth

Rift Safari Tours now receives over 200 high-quality inquiries per month purely from organic search, saving them KSh 400k/mo in ad costs.

UX Design: We redesigned their tour pages to include "Live Availability" and "Expert Video Overviews." Adding a "Chat with a Guide" WhatsApp button increased the lead-to-booking conversion rate by 22%.

The Victory: They are now a top-3 choice for luxury safaris in Kenya, competing head-to-head with brands 100x their size, all thanks to a smarter digital strategy.
