How We Increased a Local Client's Sales by 340% with Social Media

By Kim Graphics Team • May 10, 2024 • 10 min read

Social media in Kenya is no longer just for personal connections; it's a multi-billion shilling marketplace. This year, we took a local fashion boutique in Nairobi from "struggling to get likes" to "struggling to keep up with orders." Here is the exact strategy we used to increase their sales by 340% in four months.

The Challenge: Invisible in a Crowded Feed

Our client, a high-end clothing brand based in Kilimani, had 5,000 followers on Instagram but only 2-3 sales per week coming through the app. Their content was inconsistent, their photography was low-quality, and they weren't utilizing paid ads. They were invisible to their ideal customer.

Phase 1: Content Overhaul (Visual Transformation)

First, we replaced amateur phone photos with professional studio shots and high-energy lifestyle reels. We focused on "The Nairobi Lifestyle"—showing the clothes being worn at popular local brunch spots and events. Visual quality is the biggest differentiator for Kenyan brands today.

Phase 2: The "Double-Tap" Ad Strategy

We didn't just boost posts. We built a full-funnel Meta Ads strategy:

  • Top of Funnel: Video ads showcasing the brand story to women in Nairobi aged 25-45.
  • Middle of Funnel: Carousel ads featuring specific collections to people who watched the first video.
  • Bottom of Funnel: Retargeting ads with a "Free Delivery within Nairobi" offer for people who added items to their cart but didn't buy.

Phase 3: Community Management

Kenyans buy from people they trust. We implemented a "10-minute reply rule" for all DMs and comments. By answering pricing and sizing questions instantly, we captured customers at their peak moment of interest.

The Results

By the end of Month 4, the numbers were staggering:

  • Total Sales: Increased from ~KSh 120,000/mo to ~KSh 528,000/mo.
  • Follower Growth: +12,000 organic followers.
  • Ad ROI: 5.5x return on ad spend (ROAS).
