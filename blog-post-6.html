5 Graphic Design Mistakes That Kill Conversions

By Kim Graphics Team • March 15, 2024 • 5 min read

Graphic design is more than just "making things look pretty." In marketing, design is a tool for persuasion. However, many brands in Kenya fall into traps that actually drive potential customers away. Here are the 5 most common graphic design mistakes we see and how to fix them to boost your ROI.

1. Lack of Visual Hierarchy

If everything on your flyer or website is bold and bright, then nothing stands out. You must guide the viewer's eye. Your most important message (the Headline or the Offer) should be the largest, followed by the secondary details. If a customer has to "hunt" for the price or the CTA, they've already checked out.

2. Clashing Color Palettes

Colors evoke emotions. Using too many colors or clashing tones (like neon green on bright red) creates "visual noise" that makes people anxious and less likely to trust your brand. In the Kenyan market, professional brands use limited, deliberate palettes—often 2-3 core colors that complement each other.

3. Over-Complicated Logos

A logo should be recognizable in 2 seconds or less. If your logo has too many lines, gradients, and icons, it will look like a blurry mess when scaled down for a WhatsApp profile picture or a business card. Simplicity equals memorability.

4. Using Generic Stock Photos

We've all seen the same "happy office workers" photo a thousand times. It feels fake and untrustworthy. For Kenyan audiences, using local imagery or professional custom photography makes your brand feel relatable and authentic. If you must use stock, choose high-quality, non-cliché options.

5. Ignoring Whitespace

Cramming every inch of a design with text and icons makes it unreadable. Whitespace (empty space) is what gives your design "room to breathe" and makes it look premium. Don't be afraid of empty space—it's your best friend for conversion.

Ready to fix your visuals? A small design tweak can lead to a massive jump in lead quality. Don't let bad design hold your business back.
